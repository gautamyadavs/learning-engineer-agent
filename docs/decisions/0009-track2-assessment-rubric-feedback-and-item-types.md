# 0009 — Track 2 (`assessment_quiz`) 100-Point Rubric, Dedicated Learner Feedback Dimension, 7 Item Formats, and Real-Course Calibration

- **Status:** Accepted · 2026-11-25
- **Date:** 2026-11-25
- **Builds on:** [`0003`](0003-separate-review-apply-recheck.md), [`0004`](0004-no-invented-status.md), [`0008`](0008-two-level-audit-and-prior-knowledge-workflow.md)
- **Implements:** [`docs/rubric/rubric-v0.3.json`](../rubric/rubric-v0.3.json), [`src/lib/le-scoring/scorer.ts`](../../src/lib/le-scoring/scorer.ts)

## Context

In `v0.2.1` (`ADR-0008`), Track 1 (`objectives_only`) and the cross-track Prior Knowledge Audit (`PK-1 = 6 pts`) were calibrated and locked, while Track 2 (`assessment_quiz`) held provisional weights that:
1. Placed learner feedback (`P4`) solely inside `Practice & Feedback` (`0` pts in Track 2), leaving formative and post-submission quiz feedback unscored and failing to distinguish cognitive mechanisms of MCQ feedback vs. open-ended feedback.
2. Covered only single-select MCQs (`IWF-01..19`) and Open-Ended tasks (`OE-01..11`), omitting common course/OLI assessment item types (`Check All That Apply / CATA`, `Numeric & Symbolic Math`, `Auto-Graded Short-Text / Fill-in-the-Blank`, `Ordering / Parsons`, and `Multi-Input / Dropdown Cloze / Yes-No MTF Matrix`).
3. Had not yet been stress-tested against real multi-format quizzes and OLI authoring tables from Google Drive.

## Decisions

### 1. Track 2 (`assessment_quiz`) 100-Point Dimension Budget
- **Alignment to Target LOs (`20 pts`):** `A1` (`5`), `A2` (`7`), `A4` (`3`, conditional), `A5` (`5`). `A3` (*instruction prepares learners*) defaults to `not_applicable` in standalone `assessment_quiz` reviews where instructional pages are absent. `A2` enforces the **modality-claim boundary** (`AS-02`): selected-response items can sample discrimination, debugging, or decision selection, not full authoring/coding implementation.
- **Shared Assessment Quality (`14 pts`):** `S1` (`3`), `S2` (`3`), `S3` (`4`), `S4` (`2`), `S5` (`2`, conditional).
- **Item & Scoring Quality (`34 pts`):** Equally distributed **`34 / N` pts per question** across all `N` independently scored questions. Within each question, every applicable check for its format carries equal `1 / K_q` weight (Haladyna, Downing, & Rodriguez, 2002; Brookhart, 2018). `Minor risk` observations record unscored `advisoryNotes` without deducting points (`MCQ Quality Coach`).
- **Learner Feedback & Support (`14 pts`):**
  - **Set-level (`4 pts`):** `FB-S1` (`2 pts`, purpose-matched timing & Attempt-1 / cross-item spoiler protection; Shute, 2008; Van der Kleij et al., 2015; Attali & van der Kleij, 2017) and `FB-S2` (`2 pts`, task/process focus without self-level praise or overclaimed psychological diagnosis; Hattie & Timperley, 2007; Kluger & DeNisi, 1996; `AS-07`).
  - **Format-specific (`10 pts`):** Equally distributed **`10 / N` pts per question** across format-tailored feedback criteria (`FB-M1..M4`, `FB-O1..O4`, `FB-C1..C2`, `FB-N1..N3`, `FB-T1..T3`, `FB-R1..R3`, `FB-I1..I2`).
  - **Secure-Exam `N/A` Renormalization:** Marks `not_applicable` *only* when `S1` explicitly declares a secure credentialing/placement exam that intentionally withholds learner feedback (`AS-07`), renormalizing the remaining `80` visible pts proportionally to `94` visible pts (`+ 6 PK-1 = 100`).
- **Clarity & Navigability (`12 pts`):** `C1–C5` (`2.4 pts` each when all 5 apply; `3.0 pts` each when `C5` or `C4` is `N/A`).
- **Prior Knowledge Readiness (`6 pts`):** `PK-1` (`Audit Prior Knowledge` button).
- **No Separate Unscored Distractor Panel:** To prevent split-attention clutter, target LO tags and distractor misconception repairs surface inline inside flagged item cards rather than in an extra unscored table.

### 2. Evidence-Calibrated Criteria Across All 7 Question Formats
1. **`mcq` (`IWF-01..19` + `FB-M1..M4`):** Elaborated explanation (`FB-M1`, Van der Kleij et al., 2015), correct-key principle reinforcement (`FB-M2`, Butler, Karpicke, & Roediger, 2008), option-specific distractor misconception repair neutralizing negative suggestion (`FB-M3`, Roediger & Marsh, 2005; `LLSS3 s.23, 26`), and optional non-spoiler hint progression (`FB-M4`, `LLSS3 s.39`; Aleven et al., 2016).
2. **`open_ended` (`OE-01..11` + `FB-O1..O4`):** Verifiable model/anchor without wall-of-text overload (`FB-O1`, Sadler, 1989; Lipnevich et al., 2014; Shute, 2008), criterion-linked gap diagnosis (`FB-O2`, Wollenschläger et al., 2016; Brookhart, 2018), actionable *"Where to Next"* feed-forward without overwriting student voice (`FB-O3`, Brooks et al., 2019), and partial/alternative-path notes (`FB-O4`).
3. **`cata` (`CATA-01..04` + `12` core `IWF` + `FB-C1..C2`):** `CATA-01` discourages CATA in early/introductory formative practice—where dichotomous `0/1` scoring harshly penalizes partial mastery and combinatorial `"Incorrect"` feedback frustrates novices (Brassil & Couch, 2019; Couch et al., 2018; NCSBN, 2023; `LLSS3 s.28–29`)—offering conversion to Multiple True–False (`MTF`) or focused MCQs. Retained integrative CATA items require explicit `"Select all that apply"` cues (`CATA-02`), partial credit (`CATA-03`), `3–7` options with zero `"all/none of the above"` (`CATA-04`), and per-option proposition feedback (`FB-C1`).
4. **`numeric_math` (`NUM-01..05` + `FB-N1..N3`):** Explicit units/constants (`NUM-01`), rounding & tolerance bands (`NUM-02`), separated algebraic equivalence vs. required form (`NUM-03`, Sangwin, 2013, 2023 STACK *Separated Concerns*), construct-clean equation entry syntax (`NUM-04`, Sangwin, 2010; Gratwick et al., 2020), manageable step chains (`NUM-05`, Ashton et al., 2006), concise post-attempt worked setup (`FB-N1`, McGuire et al., 2020 ASSISTments; Fyfe, 2016; Attali, 2015), and buggy-value error branches (`FB-N2`, Brown & Burton, 1978; Koedinger & Corbett, 2006).
5. **`short_text` (`TXT-01..05` + `FB-T1..T3`):** Determinate `~1–5` word target (`TXT-01`, Jordan, 2012; Mitchell et al., 2002), direct question / end-of-sentence concept blank (`TXT-02`, Haladyna, 2004; Miller, Linn, & Gronlund, 2009), synonym/acronym/inflection normalization (`TXT-03`, Jordan & Mitchell, 2009), fair typo tolerance (`TXT-04`, Horbach, 2018), zero `a/an` or box-length giveaways (`TXT-05`), and near-miss foil branches (`FB-T2`).
6. **`ordering` (`ORD-01..05` + `FB-R1..R3`):** Authentic sequential construct fit (`ORD-01`, Haynes & Ericson, 2021; `LLSS3 s.30`), unique or topological commuting-step equivalence (`ORD-02`, Denny et al., 2008), `3–7` steps avoiding unpaired distractor blocks on summative/novice items (`ORD-03`, Ericson et al., 2019, 2022; Haynes-Magyar, 2025), relative-order partial credit avoiding index-shift cliffs (`ORD-04`), non-drag click/select + keyboard accessibility (`ORD-05`, W3C WCAG 2.2 `SC 2.5.7` & `2.1.1`), and violated-pair feedback (`FB-R2`).
7. **`multi_input` (`MI-01..04` + `FB-I1..I2`):** Bounded `2–6` coherent sub-inputs (`MI-01`, `LLSS3 s.34`), part-level credit & cascading-error ("hinged") protection (`MI-02`, Yen, 1993 LID; PMC6788158; Treagust, 1988; Sangwin, 2013), independent dropdown options (`MI-03`), explicit screen-reader part labels (`MI-04`), and part-specific diagnostic feedback (`FB-I1`).

### 3. Six Real-Course Calibration Refinements Verified on 3 Drive Assessments
1. **Answer-Key Integrity Gate on `Ready` (`require_defensible_s3_keys: true`):** In `LEFP Week 2` (`6` items), 5 strong items yielded `82/100` proportional credit even though `mcq8-1` swapped KLI `Skill` and `Principle` definitions (`S3 = revise`). Adding `require_defensible_s3_keys: true` keeps the fair `82/100` numeric score while blocking the `Ready` badge until `S3` has zero wrong or unverified keys/rubrics.
2. **Default `A3 = not_applicable` in Standalone `assessment_quiz`:** Prevents penalizing standalone quizzes `4` pts when callers omit per-pair `A3` `not_applicable` markers.
3. **Substantive Feedback Accuracy + `S3` Consolidation:** Feedback checks (`FB-M2/M3`, etc.) require accurate domain explanations (catching inverted definitions and copy-pasted correct-answer text across distractors in `LEFP Week 2` `mcq8-1` & `mcq5`).
4. **OLI `LBD_TAF` / `MTF` Routing to `multi_input`:** Yes/No matrices (`dissect_skills_TF1`) normalize to `multi_input` rather than `cata`, rewarding statement-level verification without false `CATA-01` flags.
5. **Mixed `Formative (F)` / `Summative (S)` Authoring Tables + Cross-Item Spoiler Detection:** Supports OLI tables mixing `F` and `S` items (`OLI Module 6`) and flags sibling-item key leakage in shared-stimulus sets (`mcq3-1..3-3`).
6. **Blank Item `Skill` Tags (`C1` vs. `A1`) & External Unattached Rubrics (`unverified`):** Deducts missing item-level LO/Skill table tags once under `C1` when module LOs match (`OLI Module 6`), ignores Google Docs `1. 1. 1.` table numbering artifacts, and marks external unattached rubrics `unverified` (`0` pts, prompting attachment) rather than inventing rubric critiques.
